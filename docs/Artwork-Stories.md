# Artwork stories

BAETEE is a tribute to the daughters of India and the lives they have shaped in India and around the world, as stated by Malik Gilani.

The 15 notes in `lib/artwork-stories.js` describe visible motifs in the existing artwork and offer interpretations through this dedication. They do not claim documented artist intent or portray specific historical figures. `components/ProductView.jsx` displays them on T-shirt product pages under “The art you wear”, followed by the shared brand dedication.
